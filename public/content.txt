1 - Meet the Founder – Serhat Çelik

Serhat Çelik is a businessman and entrepreneur active in the fields of energy, engineering, technology, and business development, and is the founder of Green Pi Energy.
With the aim of bringing together his technical and commercial experience in the energy sector under his own vision, he founded Green Pi Energy on 2 August 2023.
The core business philosophy that Serhat Çelik has established for Green Pi is based on the principle of “creating solutions rather than simply selling products.”
Rather than merely supplying a transformer, switchgear, panel, or automation product, this approach focuses on evaluating the customer’s entire electrical infrastructure, developing the most suitable system architecture, selecting the right products, planning system integration, and ensuring continuous technical support after the sale.
For this reason, the development of Green Pi is built around the combination of engineering expertise, commercial capability, and long-term customer relationships.
From Energy to Technology
Serhat Çelik’s entrepreneurial vision is not limited to the traditional energy sector.
As of July 2026, Green Pi expanded its scope of activities into software and information technologies, with the development of tailored solutions for energy and industrial companies, including:
CRM, ERP, B2B platforms, web-based systems, and industry-specific software solutions.
In this direction, Green Pi Works was developed as a structure that brings together Serhat Çelik’s experience in the energy sector with modern software technologies.
The objective is to provide customers not only with electrical systems, but also with digital solutions that manage the sales, operations, data, and business processes associated with these systems.
In addition to the energy and software sectors, Serhat Çelik has also made investments in agriculture and livestock farming.
On 12 December 2024, Green Pi Agriculture & Livestock commenced operations in Sarıkaya, Yozgat, focusing on cattle breeding, sheep and lamb farming, as well as agricultural production for animal feed.
As a result, the Green Pi brand has gradually developed into a diversified business structure covering:
Energy + Engineering + Technology + Software + Agriculture
and continues to evolve as a multi-sector entrepreneurial group with a long-term growth vision.



2- ABOUT GREEN PI ENERGY
Green Pi Energy Consulting Electrical Industry and Trade Ltd. Co. was established on 2 August 2023 to operate in the fields of energy engineering, project development, product supply, and system integration.
Since its establishment, the company has focused particularly on Low Voltage (LV), Medium Voltage (MV), power distribution systems, and renewable energy solutions. Green Pi Energy’s approach goes beyond product sales; the company aims to deliver end-to-end engineering solutions, covering every stage from needs assessment and product selection to project design, supply, integration, commissioning, and after-sales support.
Our Fields of Activity
Green Pi Energy provides electrical distribution and engineering solutions for industrial facilities, energy projects, infrastructure investments, commercial buildings, data centers, renewable energy plants, and special engineering applications.
Our main areas of activity include:
•	Medium Voltage distribution systems
•	Low Voltage distribution systems
•	Transformer systems
•	MV switchgear and RMU systems
•	LV main and sub-distribution panels
•	Power factor correction and harmonic filtering systems
•	Power analyzers and energy monitoring systems
•	Protection relays and metering systems
•	SCADA, RTU, automation, and communication solutions
•	Earthing and lightning protection systems
•	Solar energy systems
•	Electric vehicle charging infrastructure
•	Cables and cable connection accessories
•	Power quality solutions
•	Mobile substations
•	Special engineering and turnkey energy projects
Medium Voltage Solutions
One of Green Pi Energy’s core areas of expertise is Medium Voltage systems.
Depending on project requirements and voltage levels, the company provides engineering and supply solutions for:
MV switchgear, RMU systems, circuit breaker and fused switchgear, metering panels, transformer protection panels, incoming and outgoing feeder panels, as well as protection and control systems.
Our engineering approach is not limited to equipment selection. Short-circuit withstand, protection coordination, metering architecture, communication infrastructure, operational philosophy, and future system expansion are also evaluated as part of the overall design.
Transformer Solutions
Green Pi Energy provides engineering and supply services for a wide range of transformer applications, including:
•	Hermetically sealed oil-immersed transformers
•	Conservator-type oil transformers
•	Dry-type transformers
•	Transformers designed for special power ratings and voltage levels
Transformer selection is carried out by considering not only the required power rating, but also load characteristics, environmental conditions, cooling requirements, losses, protection philosophy, short-circuit levels, and future capacity expansion.
Low Voltage Systems
Green Pi Energy develops LV distribution solutions tailored to the requirements of industrial facilities, commercial projects, and infrastructure applications.
Our solutions include:
Main Distribution Boards, Sub-Distribution Boards, MCC systems, Power Factor Correction Panels, Automation Panels, Automatic Transfer Systems, and custom-designed electrical panels.
Key design priorities include energy continuity, selectivity, operational safety, ease of maintenance, system reliability, and future expandability.
SCADA, RTU, and Energy Management
Modern energy systems must not only operate reliably; they must also be measurable, monitorable, connected, and remotely manageable.
Green Pi Energy provides digitalization and energy management solutions based on technologies such as:
•	SCADA systems
•	RTU systems
•	PLC applications
•	Power analyzers
•	Smart meters
•	Protection relays
•	IEC 61850
•	Modbus
•	RS485
•	Ethernet/IP-based communication systems
The objective is to enable customers to monitor energy consumption and system status in real time, identify failures more quickly, improve operational efficiency, and make better decisions based on accurate system data.
Renewable Energy
Green Pi Energy is also active in renewable energy projects, which represent a key part of the global energy transition.
In solar energy applications, the company provides product and engineering solutions in areas including:
•	AC and DC distribution systems
•	1500 VDC equipment
•	DC protection products
•	Surge protection systems
•	MC4 connectors and connection equipment
•	LV/MV grid connection systems
•	Transformers
•	MV switchgear
•	Energy monitoring and metering systems
Our objective is to support renewable energy projects with reliable electrical infrastructure and integrated power distribution solutions.
Mobile Substation Solutions
One of the key engineering areas currently being developed by Green Pi Energy is Mobile Substation Solutions.
Within a mobile substation platform, Green Pi Energy can integrate:
MV switchgear, transformers, LV distribution systems, power factor correction, SCADA systems, auxiliary power supplies, generators, DC systems, protection systems, and the complete mechanical infrastructure.
These solutions are particularly suitable for temporary power requirements, emergency power supply, mining operations, large infrastructure projects, construction sites, utility applications, and facilities where continuity of power supply is critical.
Our Engineering Approach
The fundamental difference of Green Pi Energy is that we operate with a solution-oriented rather than purely product-oriented approach.
For each project, our objective is not only to determine which product the customer should purchase, but also to evaluate:
the most suitable system architecture, the correct product selection, protection philosophy, system monitoring requirements, communication infrastructure, operational reliability, and future expansion possibilities.
For this reason, Green Pi positions itself not merely as an electrical equipment supplier, but as a technical solution provider and long-term engineering partner for its customers.
Strong Brand and Product Ecosystem
Green Pi Energy is capable of combining solutions from various international and local manufacturers according to the technical and commercial requirements of each project.
Brands and product groups evaluated and used within our projects include solutions from:
Schneider Electric, ABB, Siemens, LS Electric, EAE, Jean Müller, TE Connectivity, Schrack, Inotel, Miltera, Iskra, Astor, as well as various specialized manufacturers operating in different segments of the electrical and energy industry.
This multi-brand structure provides Green Pi with the flexibility to develop the most technically suitable and commercially competitive solution, rather than being limited to a single manufacturer or product family.
Software and Digitalization
As of July 2026, Green Pi Energy expanded its corporate scope to include software and information technology solutions.
Within this framework, the company has started developing solutions for the energy and industrial sectors, including:
•	CRM systems
•	ERP systems
•	B2B platforms
•	Web-based management systems
•	Custom software development
•	Software solutions specifically designed for panel builders and industrial companies
•	Quotation, sales, and customer management systems
Through this structure, Green Pi aims to combine its experience in the energy sector with software technologies and provide its customers not only with electrical infrastructure, but also with digital business infrastructure.
Our Vision and Long-Term Goal
Green Pi Energy’s long-term objective is to become an international solution company that brings together energy, engineering, technology, and digitalization under one roof.
In addition to its operations in Türkiye, the company aims to expand its engineering, product supply, system integration, and project development activities across Europe, the Middle East, the Caucasus, and surrounding markets.
For Green Pi, growth does not simply mean selling more products.
Our core objective is to build an organization that:
understands the customer’s technical challenges, combines the right products with the right engineering, responds quickly, provides strong after-sales support, and establishes long-term business partnerships.
Green Pi Energy aims to become a trusted partner throughout the entire project lifecycle — from the initial concept and engineering stage to supply, installation, commissioning, operation, and future expansion.
